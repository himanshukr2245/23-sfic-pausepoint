## 2. Google Form Submission Text Blocks (Word-Count Verified)

### 📌 Problem Statement (~115 words)
> Students and families in our community face predatory scam messages and deceptive phone calls every week. Bengaluru’s fastest-growing cybercrimes directly target students and young jobseekers: part-time job/task frauds surged tenfold from 228 cases in 2022 to 2,158 in 2025, and fake investment scams rose from 183 to 2,648. Both trap victims into paying repeated deposits to "unlock" fake earnings. Bengaluru lost ₹5,059 Crore to cybercrime between 2022 and June 2026; police placed liens on ₹1,810 Crore but returned only ₹414 Crore (Deccan Herald, Aug 2026). Money can only be held if victims report swiftly and accurately to the 1930 helpline with their 12-digit bank UTR, but in panic, most citizens cannot find their UTR or freeze in confusion.

### 📌 Proposed Solution (~115 words)
> PausePoint is a 100% on-device, private, offline-first web application. Its hero module, **Report**, allows a panicking victim to share a payment screenshot or bank SMS; on-device OCR extracts the amount, bank UTR, payee, and timestamp in seconds. It provides an immediate 1930 telephonic script, a multi-payment timeline for task scams, an acknowledgement SMS reader, and an automated complaint draft for cybercrime.gov.in. Its **Before You Pay Again** module halts extortionate "withdrawal fee" demands with an immediate cognitive brake. Its **Pause** module decodes psychological manipulation and link fraud. Built with high-contrast senior citizen modes, regional voice readouts, and zero cloud data transmission, ensuring total privacy.

### 📌 Potential Impact (~75 words)
> Piloted with JAIN University students, faculty families, and senior citizens. We evaluate impact through a paired timed reporting drill (measuring seconds to report-ready with vs. without PausePoint), scam recognition pre/post-session scores, and field accuracy across real payment receipts. Rapid, complete 1930 reporting dramatically increases the probability of putting bank liens on stolen funds before mule accounts disperse them, while the repeat-payment brake stops students from compounding losses.

### 📌 Feasibility (~75 words)
> The core engine is fully implemented with 79 passing automated unit tests under strict TypeScript. Because OCR and threat intelligence execute entirely on the user's mobile browser via WebAssembly and Web Crypto, marginal server infrastructure cost is ₹0, enabling unlimited community scalability. The team consists of four undergraduate engineers with proven hackathon track records (winning 3rd Prize in Human Firewall 2026), working under faculty mentorship.

## 3. Technical & Regulatory Compliance Checklist

- [x] **NCRP Jan 2026 SOP Compliance**: Validates 14-digit acknowledgement SMS starting with digit `3`.
- [x] **12-Digit Bank UTR Isolation**: Distinguishes bank UTRs from PhonePe/GooglePay internal order IDs.
- [x] **Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023**: Generates SHA-256 cryptographic digests on-device for digital evidence integrity.
- [x] **100% On-Device Privacy**: 0 bytes transmitted to any backend during OCR extraction or threat classification.
- [x] **Universal Offline Shell**: Fully functional PWA via Service Worker cache in airplane mode.
- [x] **Senior Citizen Accessibility**: 22px typography and 64px touch target floors.
