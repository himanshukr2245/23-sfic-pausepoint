### [00:00 – 00:20] Hook: The Bengaluru Crisis
*(Visual: Screen showing Deccan Herald headline: "₹5,059 Crore Lost in Bengaluru Cybercrime" and 10x rise in student task scams).*

Because the Golden Hour is lost. When citizens panic, 64% cannot find their 12-digit bank UTR to give to the 1930 helpline. 
> Meet **PausePoint Golden Hour** — a sovereign citizen cognitive firewall that takes a victim from panic to a complete police report in under 60 seconds, 100% on their own device with zero cloud transmission."

### [00:20 – 00:50] The Hero Crisis Flow: /report
*(Visual: Mobile screen in Airplane Mode. User uploads a Google Pay screenshot showing a fraudulent transfer).*

> "Watch this in Airplane Mode with zero internet. The victim drops their payment screenshot. Our on-device WebAssembly OCR instantly normalizes the canvas, extracts the exact amount, isolates the crucial 12-digit Bank UTR, identifies the recipient UPI handle, and handles optical noise.
> Immediately, it launches a 1-tap dialer for helpline 1930 and displays an exact spoken script:
> *'My name is Rahul. I am reporting an online task fraud of ₹15,000 sent via Google Pay. Bank UTR is 429812345678...'*
> It even speaks the script aloud for panicking victims or elderly relatives."

### [00:50 – 01:15] Multi-Payment Ledger, Acknowledgement SMS & SHA-256 Docket
*(Visual: Showing the Multi-Payment Timeline adding 3 deposits, parsing a 14-digit SMS starting with 3, and generating the SHA-256 docket).*

> "Task scams involve multiple deposits. Our ledger automatically compounds the loss across transfers. 
> Once the 1930 call ends, our SMS parser reads the 14-digit acknowledgement number starting with 3—compliant with the January 2026 NCRP SOP—and generates a complete factual complaint narrative ready to paste into cybercrime.gov.in. 
> Simultaneously, it computes on-device SHA-256 cryptographic fingerprints for every receipt as an integrity aid under the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023."

### [01:15 – 01:40] "Before You Pay Again": The Cognitive Brake
*(Visual: Navigating to /verify. Pasting: 'Pay ₹6,750 clearance tax to withdraw your ₹45,000 earnings'. Screen triggers bright red STOP alert).*

> "Next, our repeat-payment brake halts secondary extortion. Scammers frequently demand a 'tax' or 'fee' to unlock fake earnings. 
> PausePoint instantly fires a STOP alert, plays audio warnings, and reinforces the Three Permanent Truths: legitimate platforms never charge you to withdraw your own money. 
> It also provides a Trusted Family Shield with secret safety passphrases to defeat AI deepfake kidnapping calls."
